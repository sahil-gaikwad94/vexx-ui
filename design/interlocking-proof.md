# Interlocking Proof — a visual philosophy for vexx-ui 2.1

A verification is not a verdict; it is a fit. This movement treats correctness as geometry:
many small, uncertain pieces arrive from the model, and only the ones whose edges agree are
allowed to lock. The finished object is not drawn — it is assembled. Every joint is a small
proof, and the shape that survives is the only shape that could have survived.

Form is built from **interlocking tiles with generous tabs and clean notches**, cropped by the
frame so the composition continues beyond what can be seen. No tile floats in isolation: each
one owes its position to a neighbour. A single tile sits slightly apart, its gap glowing — the
illegal token that was refused admission. The viewer should feel the near-miss before reading a
single label.

Matter is **stipple**: thousands of minute marks, each insignificant, together describing a
volume. Density is light. Bright faces dissolve into fine dust; shadowed faces collect into
heavier grains. The effect must look painstaking — the product of patient, repeated decisions,
as if someone placed every mark by hand. There is no gradient smoothness here; the shading is
earned one dot at a time. Precision is the subject and also the method.

Colour is disciplined: electric cobalt for the field, ice and near-white for verified faces, a
single lime tile for the deterministic splice, and lavender for the field of potential tokens.
The palette never becomes decoration; each hue names a state in the machine. Against the deep
blue, negative space is not empty — it is the set of tokens that were masked away.

Composition is confident and off-centre. The sculpture rises from the lower edge and is cut by
the right edge, leaving the upper-left quiet for a single declarative line of type. Text is a
rare, small instrument: a coordinate, a state label, a version. Information lives in the fit of
the pieces, the grain of the light, and the one gap that refuses to close. The whole image must
read as the work of someone at the top of their field — meticulously crafted, unhurried, and
utterly sure of itself.
